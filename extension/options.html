LOCAL BROWSER CONTROL

Universal Agent Browser Bridge

Only tabs marked ON are visible to paired agents. Click the toolbar icon on a tab to share or revoke it.

Agent access

Skip click and Enter approval only in tabs created by the calling agent's label; hand-shared tabs still ask.

One domain per line. Each domain and its subdomains are blocked from agent access, including hand-shared tabs.

Safety boundary

  • The bridge listens only on your computer.
  • Agents see only tabs you explicitly share or let them create.
  • Bank, card, payment and password-manager pages stay read-only, even in trusted tabs.
  • Creating the agent window always asks for approval.
  • Remove a tab by clicking the toolbar icon again.
  • Do not share banking, password-manager, or payment tabs.
